Frequently asked questions

Most Schengen applications need a valid passport, the application form, photographs, travel medical insurance, flight and accommodation reservations, bank statements and proof of employment or study. Extra documents depend on your reason for travelling. We give you a personalised checklist so you know exactly what applies to you.

We recommend contacting us [6 to 8 weeks] before you travel. Applications can usually be submitted up to six months before your trip and no later than 15 days before. Appointments fill up quickly in busy seasons, so earlier is always calmer.

Embassies usually aim to decide within about 15 calendar days of your appointment, but this can take longer in busy periods. Processing times are set by the embassy, not by us.

Yes. We assist with booking a suitable appointment and make sure you know what to bring, where to go and what to expect on the day.

If you are visiting family, friends or a business, an invitation letter from your host is usually requested. We guide you and your host on what it should include.

Most Schengen applications require you to attend your appointment in person so that your fingerprints and photograph can be taken. We prepare you fully so that you arrive organised and confident. [Confirm whether any submission support is offered.]

No one can guarantee a visa. Approval is solely at the discretion of the relevant embassy, consulate or immigration authority. Our role is to help you present a complete, accurate and well organised application.

We do not provide legal or immigration advice, we do not make decisions on applications, and we never create or alter supporting documents such as bank statements or letters. Every document must be genuine and your own.

We handle your personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). We only ask for what is needed, share it only for your application, and explain how to send documents securely. Please do not send passport or ID details through the website form.

Our fees depend on the support you need and the number of applicants. Send us your travel plans and we will reply with a clear quote. Embassy, visa centre and insurance fees are separate and paid directly. [Confirm pricing approach.]
